How to use this pathway

New to accessibility at MNIT-DOT? Begin with Section 1 (trainings 1 to 3). Afterward, it’s best to complete Sections 2 to 5 in sequence, but you can choose the order based on your work. For instance, a program manager handling vendor management might proceed to Section 3, while a business analyst working on requirements might move on to Section 2.
