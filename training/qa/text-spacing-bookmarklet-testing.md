Users with low vision, dyslexia, or cognitive disabilities often increase line height, letter spacing, and word spacing to reduce visual crowding. WCAG 1.4.12 (Text Spacing) requires that content stay readable and functional when they do.

This lesson shows QA analysts how to use the Text Spacing Bookmarklet, which applies the WCAG values in one click: line height 1.5 times the font size, paragraph spacing 2 times, letter spacing 0.12 times, and word spacing 0.16 times. You'll learn what to look for on pages, forms, and components such as cards, accordions, tabs, and modals: overlapping or cut-off text, labels that shift away from inputs, hidden overflow, and misaligned controls.
