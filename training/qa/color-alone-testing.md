Many users have low vision, color blindness, or contrast sensitivity. If color is the only indicator of meaning, such as a required field marked only in red or success and failure shown only as green and red, those users miss the information. WCAG 1.4.1 (Use of Color) requires that color support meaning but never be its only source.

This lesson shows QA analysts what to look for: required fields, error states, active or inactive states, links, and chart categories that depend on color alone. You'll learn what a passing pattern looks like (text labels, icons, patterns or shapes, legends with clear text) and how to check forms and error messages so a red border is always paired with a descriptive message.
