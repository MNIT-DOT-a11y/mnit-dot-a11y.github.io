This lesson sets the shared foundation for every QA lesson that follows. Accessibility on our teams means every person can use our applications regardless of ability, device, or assistive technology. It is part of our development standards and testing expectations, and QA is responsible for verifying it through manual and automated testing.

You'll meet the four POUR principles (Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, Robust) that organize WCAG, the standard we test against. The lesson also looks at who we test for: screen reader and magnifier users, people using high contrast, speech input, or alternative keyboards, and anyone with a temporary or situational limitation. It closes with where QA fits in the lifecycle and why accessibility is a mindset, not just a checklist.
