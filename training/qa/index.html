How to use this pathway

New to accessibility at MNIT-DOT? Start with Section 1 and work through the lessons in order. Each builds on the previous one. Supervisors and leads can use the sections as an onboarding roadmap. Already comfortable with the basics? Jump to the testing skill or language integration you need.

Each training links to the corresponding How-to Test guides on this site, so you can move straight from a lesson to the step-by-step test for a component.

Each lesson includes a summary, a training video, a downloadable slide deck, and a knowledge check to assess your comprehension before moving on. Each lesson takes about 10 to 15 minutes. Proceed through the sections in order or choose a specific topic you need.
