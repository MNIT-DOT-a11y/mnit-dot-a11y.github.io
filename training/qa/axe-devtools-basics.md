Axe DevTools is one of the core tools QA uses at MNIT DOT because it aligns with WCAG 2.1 AA and finds a large share of common issues in seconds. This lesson takes you from installing the browser extension to running your first full-page scan and reading the results.

You'll learn the difference between Violations, Needs Review, and Best Practices; how each finding shows the WCAG criterion, an explanation, the failing code, and a highlight on the page; and how to prioritize high-impact issues. Because Axe only catches roughly 30 to 40 percent of accessibility problems, the lesson also covers pairing scans with keyboard, screen reader, zoom, and color testing, and recording findings so developers can act on them.
