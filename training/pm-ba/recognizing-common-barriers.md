This training helps PMs and BAs spot accessibility red flags during routine delivery activities, including reviewing wireframes and requirements, participating in backlog refinement, watching sprint demonstrations, and attending vendor walkthroughs. It does not train participants to perform a conformance evaluation or technical accessibility test.
