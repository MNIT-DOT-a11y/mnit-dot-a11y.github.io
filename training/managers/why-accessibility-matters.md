This training provides MNIT-DOT staff with a clear understanding of digital accessibility, why it matters, and the legal and policy frameworks that guide our work. It explains how accessibility affects all digital products and services, including websites, applications, documents, and tools used both publicly and internally, and reinforces that accessibility is mandatory, not an optional enhancement.
