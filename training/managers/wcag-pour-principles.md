This training explains WCAG 2.1 AA as a shared framework for reducing accessibility risk and building usable digital services. WCAG is organized around four principles: Perceivable, Operable, Understandable, and Robust (POUR), and Level AA conformance requires meeting both Level A and Level AA success criteria.
