How to use this pathway

New to accessibility at MNIT-DOT? Start with Section 1 (trainings 1 to 3). Afterward, it’s best to complete Sections 2 to 4 in order, though you can choose the order based on your needs. Section 5 is best completed after at least one other section is complete. For instance, champion-building, escalation, and vision communication land better once a manager has already thought through their team’s people and process gaps.

Each lesson includes a summary, a training video, a downloadable slide deck, and a knowledge check to assess your comprehension before moving on. Each lesson takes about 15 to 45 minutes.
